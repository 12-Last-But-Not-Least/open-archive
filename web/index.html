How it works

  1. Create your profile. Sign in with Bluesky and add your name and institution.
  2. Bring your lab together. Create a lab, then share an invitation link with its members.
  3. Add your publications. Search for your papers and select the ones you want to share. Your lab page collects its members’ papers when visitors open or refresh it.
