Make an Account
  1. Create an account at Bluesky, or use the one you already have.
  2. Open Account, enter your Bluesky handle (for example, yourname.bsky.social), and click Sign in. Complete sign-in on Bluesky.
  3. Under Add papers, search your full name and choose your name from the authors shown. This creates your public researcher profile.
  4. Check the papers that are yours, then click Add selected papers.

Joining a lab? Open its invitation link, sign in, choose your author name, and click Join lab.
