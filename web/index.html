Make an Account
  1. Create an account at Bluesky, or use the one you already have.
  2. Open Account, enter your Bluesky handle (for example, username.bsky.social), and click Sign in. Complete sign-in on Bluesky.
  3. Open Create or edit your researcher profile and save your name and institution. You can also create your profile by choosing your author name under Add papers.
  4. Check the papers that are yours, then click Add selected papers.

Joining a lab? Open its invitation link, sign in, create your researcher profile, and click Join lab.
